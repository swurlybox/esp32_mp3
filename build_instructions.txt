. /opt/esp-idf/export.sh

idf.py set-target esp32
idf.py build
idf.py -P /dev/ttyUSB0 flash monitor

TIP! : Do not flash with IO lines connected to the peripherals! Keep the ESP
    unconnected, w/ just the usb cable hooked up, do the flash/monitor, then
    hook up your peripherals, and hit the reset button.

    If that doesn't work, try detaching the VIN and GND pins. Reset the MCU.
    Then reattach the VIN and GND pins. Then reset the MCU again.

    Connect your peripherals to the same power source as your ESP32, such
    that they all power up at around the same time. I tried powering them
    separately, but ended up with device initialization errors. As such, when
    programming, connect the peripherals to the ESP32 board's 5 VIN.

    These cheap ESP32 boards have their issues, and this is one of them. Very
    inconvenient, it must have something to do with strapping boot pins. It
    doesn't even help either that the board I bought is a clone, which doesn't
    come with any datasheet or board manual, so I have to take a leap of faith.
